Scope: whole Android app (Operate mode). Audience: one job seeker on a phone, short sessions. Task: build, score, tailor, export ATS CVs.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The CV is a lump of clay you press into shape until it's "ready". Refuses the category default of a grey form wizard with a blue progress bar.
OWN-WORLD: Willow-tinted ground (#E9F1E4), Turf Green owns the shell and primary actions, Lemon Chiffon is the paper the CV lives on, Scarlet Rush is the "full stop" accent (score dot, missing keywords, destructive), Dark Coffee is all ink and the dark theme ground. Soft extruded clay: dual shadows (coffee-tinted drop + light top-left highlight), 24-28dp radii, inner-shadow press state. Bricolage Grotesque display, Figtree body.
STORY: User sees their CVs as clay sheets, opens one, fills sections in short cards, watches the Readiness Stamp rise, tailors against a job post, exports a plain ATS PDF.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: Turf clay slab header with greeting + best CV's Readiness Stamp; below, CV sheets list (Lemon cards) with score dot; one FAB "CV baru"; bottom navigation bar (CV Saya / Cocokkan / Akun).
FORM: pinned by brief (claymorphism + palette); Material navigation and controls themed in clay. Signature move: the Readiness Stamp — a clay disc whose fill rises and squashes like the logo's full stop when the ATS score changes.
seed: none (direction pinned by the user's brief; no roll was run)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
